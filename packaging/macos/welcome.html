HEXR Companion

Test HEXR haptic gloves from your Mac: scan, connect a left and/or right glove, drive any channel, watch live pressure and battery, and run a pass/fail sweep on all six channels.

This installs HEXR Companion into your Applications folder. It is the app that used to be called HEXR Window Tester; if you have that one, you can move it to the Bin once this is installed.

The first time you scan for gloves, macOS will ask whether the app may use Bluetooth. Choose Allow.
